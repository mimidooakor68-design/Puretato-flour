Where is my batch?

Each batch goes through four steps. The amber bar and the green line show the step it is on today.

  • 1. Washing and slicing: the potatoes are cleaned and cut into thin slices.
  • 2. Soaking: the slices are soaked to keep them clean and white.
  • 3. Drying: the slices are dried until no moisture is left, then milled into flour.
  • 4. Ready for pickup: the bags are packed and waiting for you.
